Job Title:
Junior Data Scientist

About the Role:
We are looking for a Junior Data Scientist to join our analytics team. You will clean and explore data, build simple machine learning models, and present your findings to the business team.

Required Skills:
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Machine Learning (scikit-learn)
- SQL
- Data Visualization (Matplotlib or Seaborn)

Experience:
1-2 years of experience in data science, data analysis, or a similar role. Internships and university projects count.

Education:
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, or a related field.

Languages:
English (good written and spoken communication).

Certifications:
No certification is required.

Optional Skills (nice to have):
- Deep Learning (TensorFlow or PyTorch)
- Power BI or Tableau
- Git and GitHub
- Experience with cloud platforms
- A data science certificate (for example Google Data Analytics or IBM Data Science)
